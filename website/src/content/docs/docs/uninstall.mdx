---
title: Uninstall Reqly
description: Remove Reqly, its certificate and its data from your Mac or PC, and from your devices.
---

import { Tabs, TabItem } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

## On your computer

<Tabs syncKey="os">
<TabItem label="Mac">

1. Stop capturing.
2. Remove the helper: in **Reqly › Settings… › General**, click **Remove Helper…**, then **Remove Helper**.
3. Remove the certificate: in **Settings › HTTPS**, click **Remove Certificate…**, then **Remove Certificate**, and enter your password.
4. Quit Reqly, and drag it from **Applications** to the Trash.

If you installed Reqly with Homebrew, remove the helper and the certificate first, then run:

```bash
brew uninstall --zap --cask reqly
```

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows">

1. Quit Reqly: choose **File › Quit Reqly** (Ctrl+Q), or **Quit Reqly** in its panel in the notification area. Closing its window can leave it running there.
2. In Windows' **Settings › Apps › Installed apps**, find Reqly, and uninstall it.
3. If you installed Reqly's certificate, Windows asks you to confirm removing it. Confirm, so Windows stops trusting it. If you say no, the certificate stays.

Uninstalling also puts your proxy settings back if they still point at Reqly, and removes Reqly from File Explorer's choices for `.reqly` and `.har` files. There's no helper to remove.

To remove only the certificate, and keep Reqly: in **Settings › HTTPS**, click **Remove…**, then **Remove certificate**, and confirm when Windows asks.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

### Reqly's data

<Tabs syncKey="os">
<TabItem label="Mac">

These stay on your Mac until you delete them:

- `~/Library/Application Support/Reqly`: your rules, connections, devices and the list of `.proto` files.
- `~/Library/Preferences/net.reqly.Reqly.plist`: your settings.
- In your keychain, if you used them: **Reqly Upstream Proxy**, the upstream proxy's password, and one **Reqly Client Certificate** item for each client certificate. Delete them in Keychain Access.

Homebrew's `--zap` deletes the folder and the settings, but not the keychain items.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows">

These stay on your PC until you delete them, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Reqly`:

- `Settings.json`: your settings.
- `Rules.json`, `Connections.json`, `Devices.json` and `Protobuf.json`: your rules, connections, devices and the list of `.proto` files.
- `Secrets.dat`, if you used them: the upstream proxy's password and the client certificates' keys, encrypted for your Windows account.
- `Certificate.dat`, if you kept Reqly's certificate: the certificate and its key.
- `Reqly.log` and the `Crashes` folder: what Reqly did, and its crash reports.

To delete them all, type `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Reqly` in File Explorer's address bar, and delete that folder.

If you kept the certificate, it's still among the ones Windows trusts for you. To remove it, open the certificate manager, `certmgr.msc`, and look for **Reqly CA** under **Trusted Root Certification Authorities**, then **Certificates**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## On your devices

- **iPhone and iPad:** remove Reqly's profile in **Settings › General › VPN & Device Management**, and set the Wi-Fi network's proxy back to **Off**.
- **Android:** remove Reqly's certificate in its security settings, and set the Wi-Fi network's proxy back to **None**.
- **Android emulators:** in the Devices window, click **Stop Using Reqly** on the Mac, or **Stop using Reqly** on Windows, before you remove Reqly.
