# Code of conduct

Reqly is made in the open, and everyone who takes part is welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity. This applies in issues, pull requests and anywhere else the project lives, and when someone speaks for the project elsewhere.

## What's expected

- Be kind and patient, with newcomers most of all.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with an idea by saying why.
- Accept that a maintainer may decide differently than you would, and may say no to a change.
- Keep other people's private information private.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, harassment, and demeaning comments about anyone.
- Sexual language or imagery, and attention someone doesn't want.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Pressing a point after you've been asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior troubles you, contact the maintainer, [@parsamlm](https://github.com/parsamlm), privately, through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Say what happened, where and when, with links if you have them. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, warn the person, or block them from the project for a while or for good.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
